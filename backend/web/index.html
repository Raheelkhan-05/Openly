    How to read the results

    An open means the tracking image loaded. That does not prove anyone read the message, so each result says how sure Openly is.

    High
    A link was clicked, or a real mail app loaded the image.
    Medium
    Gmail or Yahoo loaded it for the reader. If you looked at your own sent mail on your phone, pause counting from the toolbar popup or use "That was me".
    Low
    Only scanners or preloads so far. These never count as a read.

    When one email goes to several people, an open can't be matched to a person. A forwarded email can be opened by someone you didn't write to; Openly shows when it sees more than one device.
