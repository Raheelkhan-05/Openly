## What Openly stores about you (the sender)
- Your email address, used only to sign you in.
- For each tracked email: the subject, the recipient addresses, the links you chose to track, and when you sent it.
- Your settings.

Openly does **not** read, store or upload the body of your emails or your inbox.

## What Openly stores about recipients
When a recipient opens a tracked email or clicks a tracked link, Openly records the time, the type of browser or mail app, and a shortened, salted fingerprint of the network address (never the address itself). This is used only to show you the activity and to tell people apart from scanners.

## What we don't do
We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use it for anything except running Openly for you.

## Your control
Export everything or delete everything at any time from Settings > Your data. Deleting your data removes it from our database.
