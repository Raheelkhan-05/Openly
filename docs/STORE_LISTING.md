# Chrome Web Store listing text

**Name:** Openly: email open tracking for Gmail

**Short description (under 132 characters):**
See when your Gmail emails are opened or clicked, with honest confidence levels and no mix-ups with your own views.

**Detailed description:**
Openly tells you when the emails you send from Gmail are opened, and how sure it is.

- Open and click tracking, with notifications
- Knows the difference between a person, a scanner and you. Your own views are left out
- Pause counting while you check your sent mail on your phone, or tap "That was me"
- Confidence levels (high, medium, low) instead of a misleading green tick
- Follow-up reminders when an email hasn't been opened
- Insights: when people open, and how fast
- No "Sent with Openly" line added to your emails
- Export or delete all your data at any time

**Single purpose:** Show the sender when emails they sent from Gmail were opened or clicked.

**Permission explanations (paste into the review form):**
- `https://mail.google.com/*`: needed to add the tracking image and links to the email you are writing, and to notice when you view your own sent email. Openly does not read or store email bodies.
- `storage`: saves your settings and sign-in on your device.
- `alarms`: checks for new opens once a minute while Chrome is running.
- `notifications`: shows a notification when an email is opened or clicked.
- `declarativeNetRequest`: stops your own browser from loading the tracking image while you write the email, so you don't count as an open.

**Category:** Productivity
