    What Openly can and can't tell you

    An open means the tracking image was loaded. It doesn't prove anyone read the message. Openly shows how sure it is.

    • High: a link was clicked, or a real mail app loaded the image.
    • Medium: Gmail or Yahoo loaded the image for the reader. If you checked your sent mail on your phone, pause counting from the toolbar popup or use "That was me".
    • Low: only scanners or preloads so far, which never count as a read.
    • When one email goes to several people, an open can't be matched to a person.
    • Forwarded emails can be opened by people you didn't write to. Openly shows when it sees more than one device.
